Market knowledge

Explore global markets.

A beginner-friendly overview of major asset classes. This page is educational; it does not provide prices or trading access.

01 / FX

Forex

The foreign exchange market involves buying one currency while selling another. Exchange rates can move quickly and losses are possible.

02 / DIGITAL

Cryptocurrency

Digital assets use cryptographic networks. Prices can be highly volatile and platforms carry technology and custody risks.

03 / METALS

Precious metals

Gold and silver are traded in several forms, each with different pricing, storage and market risks.

04 / EQUITIES

Stocks

Shares represent ownership interests in companies. Their values can rise or fall with company and market conditions.

05 / INDEX

Indices

Indices measure the performance of a selected group of securities using defined methodologies.

06 / RAW MATERIALS

Commodities

Energy, metals and agricultural products are influenced by supply, demand, weather and global events.
