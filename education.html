Learn responsibly

Financial knowledge starts here.

1. Understand risk

All investing and trading involves risk. You can lose some or all of the money you invest. Never rely on guaranteed-return claims.

2. Know the product

Learn how an asset works, what affects its price, and what fees, spreads or other costs may apply before considering any financial product.

3. Recognise scams

Be cautious of unsolicited messages, pressure to act quickly, requests to transfer funds to personal accounts, and promises of certain profits.

4. Check regulation

Before using any financial provider, independently verify its legal name, regulatory permissions and official contact details with the relevant regulator.
