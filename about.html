Current business status:

VELTRONIX is currently a concept and educational website prototype, not a licensed or authorised brokerage. It does not provide investment services, accept customer funds or execute trades.

Any future real-money brokerage operation would require qualified adult leadership, professional legal advice, appropriate regulatory authorisations, compliance systems and suitable financial infrastructure.
